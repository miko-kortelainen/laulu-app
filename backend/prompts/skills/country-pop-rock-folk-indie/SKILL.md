# Country, Pop, Rock, Folk & Indie Lyric Craft

## 1. Narrative Progression & "Small Moment -> Big Message"
- **Show in the Verses, Crystallize in the Chorus:**
  - **Verses ("Small Moment"):** Ground the listener in concrete sensory details—specific rooms, street names, weather, physical objects, and direct dialogue that show the emotion in action without naming it abstractly.
  - **Chorus ("Big Message"):** Open up the melodic and emotional lens to deliver the universal takeaway, anchored around a memorable central title phrase or vivid visual image.
- **Verse 1 to Verse 2 Progression:**
  - Verse 1 establishes the physical scene and initial tension.
  - Verse 2 must move the timeline forward, reveal a hidden complication, or shift the emotional angle so the second chorus hits with deeper weight.

## 2. Melodic Phrasing & Prosody
- **Singable Breath Points:** Write 6–9 spoken syllables per bar with steady stress placement and natural conversational breath points. Uptempo tracks require lean phrasing (5–8 syllables) without breathless compound clauses.
- **Natural Sentence Flow & Punctuation (`.` and `,`):** Avoid chopping lines into three comma-separated mini-clauses. Use natural enjambment and varied clause lengths that support the melodic contour, ending continuing clauses at line breaks with `,` and completed sentences with `.`.

## 3. Rhyme Scheme & Rhyme Stability
- **Verse Rhyme Scheme (`ABCB DEFE`):** In 8-line verses, lines 2 & 4 MUST share a true audible rhyme (`B`), and lines 6 & 8 MUST share a true audible rhyme (`E`). Leave odd lines (`1, 3, 5, 7`) unrhymed so the storytelling feels unforced and conversational.
- **Chorus Rhyme Scheme (`ABCB` or `ABAB`):** Use clean, resonant rhymes that highlight the central hook phrase, avoiding relentless `AABB` monosyllabic couplets that sound like nursery rhymes.
