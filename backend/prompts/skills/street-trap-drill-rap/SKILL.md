# Street Trap, Drill & Rap Lyric Craft

## 1. Authentic Street Voice & Concrete Storytelling
- **Specificity & Wit Over Generic Summaries:** Ground braggadocio and street narratives in hyper-specific physical details, sharp metaphors, and lived-in scenes: exact objects, neighborhood corners, vehicle trims, denominations, and real interpersonal stakes.
- **Ban Studio-Booth & Corporate Hustle Clichés:** Never fall back on amateur "rapper rapping about rapping" lines (*"i'm in the booth, the beat is knocking / i spit the truth, no need to lie"*) or startup keynote abstractions (*"build the vision, stack the plans, move in sync, count the profit"*).
- **Scene Continuity & Escalation:**
  - Anchor Verse 1 in one coherent physical scene without teleporting between indoor safes, highways, and studio booths every two bars.
  - Escalate the stakes, conflict, or narrative consequence in Verse 2 instead of repeating Verse 1's flexes in different words.

## 2. Percussive Cadence, Bounce & Syntax
- **Pocket & Contractions:** Write bouncy, hard-hitting 9–13 syllable bars that lock into the hi-hat and snare pocket using natural spoken contractions (`ain't`, `don't`, `tryna`, `gonna`, `she's`).
- **Break the Three-Comma Template:** Never construct verse bars from three mechanical 4-syllable clauses separated by commas (*"i stash the cash, i check the safe, i lock the door"*). Mix two-part setup/punchline bars, uninterrupted full-bar statements, and syncopated internal stresses.
- **Bar Punctuation (`.` and `,`):** Punctuate bar endings and internal pauses accurately—end setup bars or dependent clauses with `,` and land punchlines or completed thoughts with `.`.
- **No Fake Ad-Libs or Rhyme Crutches:** Never tack `, huh`, `, yeah`, `, uh`, or detached verb/noun padding (*"that's a fact"*, *"no cap"*, *"then switch the mode"*, *"then we share the code"*) onto line endings.

## 3. Rhyme Architecture & Multisyllabic Density
- **Full 8-Bar Rhyme Lock:** Every single line across all 8 bars of a verse must participate in an audible rhyme scheme (`AABB CCDD`, `ABAB CDCD`, or 4-bar multisyllabic runs). Never leave lines 5–8 unrhymed.
- **Multisyllabic & Internal Rhymes Over Basic Monosyllables:**
  - Prioritize **2–3+ syllable compound rhymes** (*empty shelves / built ourselves*) and **internal rhymes** (stitching rhyming vowels from the middle of the bar to the end of the bar) over flat 1-syllable nursery rhymes (*road/load/code/mode*, *high/lie/fly/try*).
  - Keep every rhyme word inside natural street and conversational register—never use stiff, bookish dictionary-scrape words (*"watch the rivals balk"*, *"chasing every load"*) to force a rhyme.
